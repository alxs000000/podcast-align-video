# Brand guide

## Name and promise

- Product name: `podcast-align-video`
- Tagline: **Audio in. Word-aligned video out.**
- One-line description: Turn English audio or one public YouTube video into a precise, word-highlighted H.264 video without recording a browser in real time.

Keep the product name lowercase and hyphenated. Prefer concrete language—audio, words, timing, rendering, checkpoints—over generic AI claims.

## Mark

The mark places five waveform bars beside three subtitle blocks. The gold middle block represents the currently focused word. Keep clear space around the mark equal to at least one subtitle block's height.

Do not rotate the mark, recolor individual waveform bars, add model-provider logos, or place it on a visually busy background.

## Palette

| Role | Color | Hex |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Background | Near black | `#090B0C` |
| Primary text | Warm off-white | `#F5F0E7` |
| Active word | Gold | `#FFC867` |
| Text on gold | Deep brown-black | `#251707` |

These are the same colors used by the renderer, so the repository and its output share one visual identity.

## Typography

Use Geist at weight 590 where it is available. For documentation and web fallbacks, use a neutral system sans-serif. Avoid decorative display faces and monospaced body copy.

## Voice

- Precise and pragmatic.
- Explain the hybrid DOM-measurement/ASS-rendering technique plainly.
- Make performance claims measurable and label hardware-specific observations.
- State platform, language, model-access, concurrency, and media-rights limitations directly.
